Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

HAZUMU is a basketball apparel brand operated by Masafumi Imamura (Tokyo, Japan). Contact: lsp.imamura@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

  • This website collects no names, addresses or payment details. We only see aggregated statistics from Google Search Console.
  • Purchases are handled by our stores — Paintory, SUZURI and Spreadshop. Each store collects and manages your order information (name, address, payment) under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it.
  • Our personalized-order form collects only the text to be printed (name, number, preferred meaning) and a contact email address. We use them solely to create your design and reply to you, and delete them after the order is complete. Players under 18: please have a parent or guardian submit the form.

3. Pinterest and social media

We use the Pinterest API to publish our own product images to our own Pinterest account. This integration accesses only the Pins and boards of our own account. It does not collect any information about visitors or other Pinterest users.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share the information we collect with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

6. Contact
